Names work in place of numbers: /arm uzi works as /arm 5. The new /names command lists them.
/car takes color=, enter, dist= and yaw=.
Answers say in plain words what happened. The target gets a notice. The setting notify_target turns it off (on by default).
A weapon goes to the other hand or to the inventory when the hand is full. Magazines go to the pockets.
Failed and dangerous commands go to the Aya log.
