A player who is banned or kicked now sees a notice with the reason, then the connection ends a moment later.
/ban acc:N bans an account by number, also when the player is offline. An offline ban needs the permission bans.offline.
Bans also refuse the address of the player. Turn it off with the setting ban_address.
/ban PLAYER [TIME] [REASON] bans the account. TIME is 30m, 12h, 7d, 2w or perm.
/unban N, /bans [PAGE] and /baninfo N manage the list. /kick PLAYER [REASON] disconnects a player, who can join again.
Every ban, unban and kick goes to the Aya log.
